# Community Code of Conduct

Muralume welcomes constructive participation from people of every background
and experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and patient.
- Discuss ideas and observable behavior rather than attacking people.
- Give and receive technical feedback in good faith.
- Protect other people's privacy and remove personal media, paths, credentials,
  bookmarks, and device information from reports.
- Respect project scope, licensing, and maintainer decisions even when you
  disagree.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, publishing private information, sustained disruption, or
other conduct that makes participation unsafe is not acceptable in project
spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not discuss sensitive conduct reports in a public issue. Contact the
maintainer privately through GitHub, or use GitHub's abuse-reporting tools when
appropriate. Reports will be reviewed in context, and maintainers may edit or
remove content, close or lock threads, reject contributions, or temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, review
comments, and project-related communication.
